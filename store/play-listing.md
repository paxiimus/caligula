**Full description** (4,000):

```
Caligula is a calorie counter that keeps everything on your phone.

Tap today's total, type what you ate, done. There is no account to create, no ads, no analytics and no AI, and the app is built without internet access.

WHAT IT DOES
• Quick add: tap the daily number, type the calories and an optional label
• One daily target, or different targets on different days such as training or work days
• Frequent foods fill in from what you log, and usual meals that fit what you have left are suggested
• Search your own foods and a built-in UK food database
• Weight trend, progress and an estimated goal date
• A reminder to lower your targets as you lose weight
• Weekly progress photos with side-by-side compare, stored privately inside the app
• Export and import your data as a file

PRIVACY
Caligula collects nothing. Your data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Android backup is turned off for the app, so export regularly if you want a copy.

OPEN SOURCE
Free and open source under the MIT licence: github.com/paxiimus/caligula

Calorie counts, trends and goal dates are estimates, not medical advice.
```

## Data safety

Google counts data as "collected" only when it leaves the device. Caligula has no internet permission and sends nothing, so:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | No |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Not asked once you answer No above |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Not asked; users delete in-app (Settings, Delete all data) or by uninstalling |

If a later version adds anything that leaves the device (sync, crash reports, analytics), update this form before releasing it.
