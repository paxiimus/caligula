Privacy policyTerms of use

Privacy policy

Caligula collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (calories, foods, weights, settings and progress photos) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. We never receive it, so we cannot see, sell or share it.

Network access

The Android release is built without the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. The app contains no code that sends data anywhere.

Camera and photos

Caligula asks for camera access only when you choose to take a progress photo, and opens your photo library only when you choose to pick one. Photos are copied into the app's private storage and are not added to your gallery unless you share or save one yourself.

Backups and exports

Android system backup is turned off for Caligula, so your data is not copied to cloud backups. On iOS, device backups are controlled by you in your system settings. When you choose Export, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet; where that file goes is your choice.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything the app holds on the device.

Children

Caligula is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes
